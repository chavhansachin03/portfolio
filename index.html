About me

          
A quick summary of where I am and how I like to work.

        

        01 / About
      

      

        

          
I'm currently in the final year of my Master of Computer Applications (MCA), building on a foundation in web development while developing a parallel focus on data analytics. I like projects with a real user on the other end — a community managing a festival, or someone who just wants their tasks organized.

          
On the development side, I work with HTML, CSS and JavaScript to build interfaces and features that actually hold up — including handling sensitive operations like client-side encryption directly in the browser. On the analytics side, I use Python, MySQL, Excel and Power BI to clean, query and visualize data so it's easier to act on.

          
I'm looking for opportunities — internship or entry-level — where I can keep doing both: building the product, and understanding the data it produces.
